# Enterprise Transformation Learning Initiative

## Challenge

A large-scale enterprise transformation required employees to understand new claims processes, responsibilities, and workflows.

The challenge was translating complex operational changes into an easy-to-understand learning experience.

## Strategy

Designed a blended learning solution combining:

- Self-paced learning
- Instructor-led sessions
- Process practice activities
- Performance support resources

## Learning Innovation

Created the SPRING mnemonic:

**S** — Sort  
**P** — Prepare  
**R** — Review  
**I** — Identify  
**N** — Notify  
**G** — Get it to close

This simplified a complex claims workflow into a memorable performance tool.

## Impact

The solution improved employee confidence by providing a structured approach to learning new responsibilities and applying new processes.
