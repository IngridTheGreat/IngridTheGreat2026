# Healthcare Technology Training Enablement

## Challenge

Healthcare technology solutions require employees and customers to understand complex systems, workflows, and operational processes.

## Strategy

Combined technical knowledge with instructional design principles to create learning experiences that improved system adoption.

## Approach

Focused on:

- Application training
- Workflow understanding
- User enablement
- Technical translation

## Impact

Helped bridge the gap between technology complexity and user confidence by converting technical concepts into practical learning experiences.
