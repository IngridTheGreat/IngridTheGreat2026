# Organizational Change Manager

## Overview

I support organizational transformation by connecting change strategy, communication, learning, and workforce adoption.

## Capabilities

- Change readiness
- Stakeholder engagement
- Adoption strategy
- Communication planning
- Change enablement

## Impact

Successful transformation happens when employees understand the change, believe in the change, and have the capability to execute the change.
