Knowledge Manager

Building knowledge ecosystems that help organizations capture, organize, share, and apply critical information.

Knowledge Management Capabilities

KNOWLEDGE STRATEGY

• develop knowledge strategies to transform complex healthcare, insurance, and technology information into accessible, structured resources that support employee performance, learning adoption, and operational consistency

• partner with subject matter experts, business stakeholders, and technical teams to capture critical knowledge, analyze information needs, and develop resources that align with organizational goals and workforce requirements

• apply instructional design and knowledge management principles to organize processes, workflows, and technical information into practical performance support tools, job aids, documentation, and learning assets

• leverage AI-enabled content development workflows and modern learning technologies to improve knowledge capture, content organization, and the scalability of learning and performance support solutions

CONTENT GOVERNANCE

• establish structured approaches for managing learning and knowledge content by organizing materials, workflows, documentation, and performance support resources to improve consistency, usability, and accessibility

• apply instructional design standards and quality review processes to ensure training materials, job aids, technical documentation, and knowledge resources are accurate, relevant, and aligned with business and learner needs

• collaborate with stakeholders to maintain content accuracy and relevance by incorporating feedback, updates, and process changes into learning and knowledge assets

• leverage AI-assisted content development workflows to support content creation, refinement, organization, and scalability while maintaining human review and subject matter validation

SME COLLABORATION

• partner with subject matter experts, business leaders, technical teams, and operational stakeholders to capture complex healthcare, insurance, and technology knowledge and translate it into effective learning solutions

• facilitate knowledge transfer from SMEs into structured learning experiences including instructor-led training, eLearning, job aids, workflow documentation, and performance support resources

• bridge the gap between technical expertise and learner understanding by simplifying complex processes, systems, and workflows into clear, role-based training materials

• build collaborative relationships with cross-functional teams to ensure learning solutions accurately reflect business processes, system functionality, and workforce performance expectations
